Who is responsible for your information?

{{ site_information.firm_name|default:'Khoboko Law Chambers' }} (“the firm”, “we” or “us”) is responsible for personal information collected through this website. This policy concerns website use and initial enquiries. If we accept instructions, additional information about handling your matter may be provided in our engagement terms.

Information we collect

  • Enquiry details: your name, email address, optional phone number, subject and message, and the time of submission.
  • Technical information: information needed to deliver and protect the site, including IP addresses, request details and security logs that may be recorded by the server or hosting provider.
  • Staff access: authorised account and session information needed to operate the private content dashboard.

Please provide only a brief, non-confidential outline. Do not submit identity documents, financial records, privileged communications or sensitive information about yourself or another person through the enquiry form.

Why we use this information

We use enquiry details to assess your request, communicate with you, identify the appropriate next step and consider whether we can accept instructions. We use technical and account information to operate the site, control access, prevent spam and unauthorised activity, and investigate security incidents.

Processing is limited to these purposes and applicable legal or professional obligations. Where consent is required, we will seek it; an enquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list. We do not sell information submitted through the enquiry form.

Confidentiality and accepting instructions

We handle enquiries with care and restrict access to people who need the information for their work. However, submitting a form does not establish a lawyer-client relationship or confirm that we can act for you. Privilege and confidentiality protections depend on the circumstances and applicable law. We will explain how to send documents securely if further information is needed.

Service providers and other disclosures

Information may be handled by providers supporting hosting, storage, maintenance and security, as needed to operate the website. We may also disclose information where required by law or court order, or where lawfully necessary to protect rights or investigate misuse. Professional duties and legal privilege may restrict disclosure.

Where a provider processes information outside Lesotho, applicable requirements for international transfers must be observed. Contact us if you need information about the processing arrangements relevant to your enquiry.

Cookies, security counters and external content

We use essential cookies for form security, staff sessions and status messages. We also store a preference in your browser’s local storage when you dismiss the legal information notice. This records only the notice version, is not used for tracking, and remains until you clear site data or a new notice version is published. Our form protection uses short-lived database counters containing keyed hashes of network or account identifiers, rather than storing those identifiers in the counters themselves. Expired counters are removed during subsequent site activity.

The website does not include advertising or analytics scripts. If a Google map is displayed, Google may receive technical information, including your IP address, and may use cookies or other identifiers under Google’s privacy policy. External images and links may also involve third-party providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Blocking essential cookies may prevent forms or staff sign-in from working.

Retention and security

We retain enquiries for as long as reasonably needed to respond, document the outcome, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal or professional obligations. Records relating to an accepted matter may have different retention requirements. Information is deleted or anonymised when it is no longer needed, subject to lawful retention and backup cycles.

We use access controls, form validation, request limits and other technical safeguards. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Please contact us through an established channel if you suspect misuse of information relating to the firm.

Your requests and concerns

Subject to applicable Lesotho law, you may ask about personal information we hold about you, request access or correction, and request deletion or object to processing where permitted. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on it, without affecting processing already carried out lawfully. We may need to verify your identity. Legal obligations, privilege and the rights of others may limit a request.

Raise privacy questions with the chambers using the contact details below. If a concern remains unresolved, you may seek guidance or make a complaint through the competent data-protection authority where applicable.
